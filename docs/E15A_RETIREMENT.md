# Evidence scope amendment: 2026-09-06

E15A is retired from the active release. The original matched-decoy comparison
and the later patient-split correction are not part of the current method
validation, figure plan, result contract or manuscript claims. E15 retains
only E15B, the within-stratum inflow-pairing shuffle control.

The original same-table comparison was selection-affected. The subsequent
split analysis had evaluable coverage of 1/10, 2/10 and 0/10 targets in LUAD,
COAD and IDC, respectively, and did not establish broad validation success.
Retirement was requested to narrow the experiment scope; it is not evidence
that these limitations disappeared or that the remaining controls prove
absolute biological dwell-time accuracy.

E15B's cohort inputs and 400 shuffle replicates per cohort are unchanged.
Median shuffled top-10 overlap remains 0.10 / 0.30 / 0.40.
Release/result manifests and active evidence contracts have been updated for
the removal; the historical generation timestamp and commit of retained
scientific results are not re-labelled as newly generated.
